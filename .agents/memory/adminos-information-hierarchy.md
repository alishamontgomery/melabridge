---
name: AdminOS information hierarchy
description: Durable product rule for keeping the platform administration experience comprehensive without becoming cluttered.
---

AdminOS should prioritize information in this order: platform overview, items needing attention, focused management destinations, platform health, then advanced operator utilities behind progressive disclosure.

**Why:** The platform owner needs broad control and immediate situational awareness, but does not want a dense dashboard where routine navigation, roadmap placeholders, testing controls, and operational alerts all compete visually.

**How to apply:** Keep the home dashboard concise and truthful. Add detailed controls to focused admin pages, link actionable metrics to those pages, remove decorative unavailable modules, and keep test/configuration tools collapsed outside the daily path.
