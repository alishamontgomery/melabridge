---
name: Ticket buyer support policy
description: Product rules for organizer contact and ticket cancellation disclosures.
---

The event organizer is the buyer's primary support contact. Every ticketed event should define a support name/email and one clear cancellation policy: no cancellations, case-by-case review, or allowed until a set time before the event.

**Why:** Leading ticket platforms make the organizer responsible for event-specific questions and refunds. Buyers need the actual policy before paying, not a generic statement that a policy exists.

**How to apply:** Show the same organizer contact and policy on the buyer page, immediately before checkout consent, and in the purchase confirmation email. Email replies and contact actions should go to the organizer.
