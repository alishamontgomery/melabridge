---
name: Launch product principles
description: Product and interaction priorities that should guide MelaBridge launch work.
---

MelaBridge should make event planning feel simple and friendly, with AI producing an editable starting plan quickly. Frequent actions should happen inline or in one step rather than through stacked cards, dialogs, and confirmation clicks. Pending tasks must never use checkmarks or wording that makes them look finished.

**Why:** The launch goal is a product people can understand quickly, keep improving over time, and convert into sustainable recurring revenue. Complexity, misleading states, and unsupported promises directly work against trust and conversion.

**How to apply:** Prefer plain outcome language, visible next actions, honest capability claims, short purchase and signup paths, mobile-first layouts, and progressive disclosure only for uncommon settings.
