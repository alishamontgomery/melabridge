---
name: Public feature claims
description: Rules for keeping MelaBridge marketing copy aligned with live end-to-end functionality.
---

Public marketing should describe organizer-side guest lists and secure invitation-link RSVP, not a guest-facing portal. Decision Center polls, shared vendor comparisons, and team approvals are roadmap items and must not appear as live cards or populated dashboard modules.

**Why:** The public dashboard preview uses demo data and can make placeholder modules look shipped; feature cards must not be treated as evidence of implementation.

**How to apply:** Before launch copy changes, compare each claim with a working route and persistence path. Prefer precise terms such as meal notes, paid amounts, vendor needs, team access, and editable starting drafts.
