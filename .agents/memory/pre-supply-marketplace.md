---
name: Pre-supply marketplace strategy
description: Product rule for making the vendor marketplace useful before enough public vendor profiles exist.
---

Never fabricate vendors, reviews, vetting, availability, packages, or recommendations to make marketplace supply look larger than it is. Empty or unmatched searches should become actionable, event-linked demand: planners can save a sourcing request or privately record a vendor they already know.

Google Search knowledge profiles can exist while Google Places Text Search omits the same service-area business. Treat that as a provider coverage gap, not proof that the business does not exist; offer a direct Google search fallback without inventing a Places card.

**Why:** Early planners may leave when the marketplace is empty, but invented supply destroys trust and creates legal and operational risk. Capturing real demand gives planners a next step and tells MelaBridge which categories and locations to recruit first.

**How to apply:** Keep public vendor listings limited to businesses that genuinely created or claimed profiles. A public website or directory mention is evidence that a business exists, not permission to create a native listing under an unknown owner. When Places returns no match, link to a direct Google business search using the user’s query and location. Clearly distinguish public profiles, private event contacts, and sourcing requests. Do not say a vendor was contacted, invited, vetted, or matched until that action actually occurred.
